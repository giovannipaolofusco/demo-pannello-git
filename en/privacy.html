3. What data, for what purpose, on what legal basis

3.1 Browsing data

As with any website, in order to function this site receives certain data that your browser transmits automatically: IP address, browser type and operating system, date and time of the request, and the page requested. Purpose: to deliver the pages you request and to keep the service secure and operational (detection of abuse, malfunctions and attempted attacks).
Legal basis: Article 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interests. The legitimate interests pursued: the controller's interest in operating the website and protecting it from abusive use, which does not override your rights because the data remains in the hosting provider's technical logs, is not consulted in order to profile you and is not combined with other information.

The website has no contact forms, so no data is submitted through these pages and there is nothing you are required or permitted to provide in order to browse. 3.3 Cookies and tools that store information on your device

The website uses technical tools only. There are no profiling cookies, no advertising, and no analytics or traffic-measurement tool of any kind. The details are set out in section 4.

4. Cookie Policy

What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser. Some are needed to make the website work (technical cookies) and do not require your consent; others measure visits or display advertising (third-party analytics and profiling cookies) and require prior, freely given consent. Alongside cookies there are similar technologies — such as localStorage and sessionStorage — which store information on your terminal equipment and are subject to the same rules (Article 122 of the Italian Personal Data Protection Code).

Which ones this website uses

Technical only. No profiling cookies, no third-party cookies, no analytics tool. This is the complete table, not an extract:

How you give and change your choice

On your first visit a banner appears allowing you to accept or reject by means of two buttons of equal prominence, placed at the same level, together with a panel for choosing category by category. Closing the banner without choosing amounts to rejection: neither scrolling the page nor continuing to browse constitutes consent.

How long your choice lasts. We record it for 186 days (six months). On expiry the banner will appear once more, to ask whether your decision still stands: this is the minimum period laid down by Decision no. 231/2021 of the Italian Data Protection Authority, which prohibits repeating the request at every access. You may of course change your mind sooner, at any time, using the button at the foot of this page.
